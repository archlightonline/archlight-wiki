01

Bosses Tab

Unified boss tracking and teleport access

The Bosses tab is the central place to check major encounters. It replaced the older Wild Boss-only view and lets players switch between Wild, Raid, and World Boss information.

Use this tab to check boss status, available locations, last kill time, next spawn time, and teleport access when the boss is currently available.

02

Wild Bosses

Spawned by killing monsters in normal hunting areas

Wild Bosses are stronger versions of regular creatures that can spawn from monster portals and hunting areas after enough creatures of that type are killed.

The Bosses tab shows the current kill count and how close the spawn is to opening. When the requirement is reached, a portal opens in that spawn or cave.

Spawn count200 killsEntry contribution20 killsCooldown10 minutes

Wild Bosses are stronger than their normal creature versions and can drop Wild Boxes. Wild Bosses can also drop Gold Nuggets at a small chance.

Wild Boss rewardWhat it means
Wild BoxesMain Wild Boss reward box. The amount depends on the strength of the boss.
Gold NuggetSmall chance from each Wild Boss.
Unique addon itemsSome Wild Bosses can drop addon-related items.
Soul ShardsWild Bosses are one of the major boss sources for Soul Shards.

Stackable, tradeable reward boxes from Wild Bosses

04

Raid Bosses

Broadcasted boss raids and event-world raid areas

Raid Bosses are broadcasted encounters that use raid portals and dedicated boss areas. When the raid starts, players enter through the available portal and are sent to the raid area instead of fighting the boss on the regular world map.

Raid areas can place players in a protection zone or randomized area depending on the encounter. After the boss dies, the area can remain open briefly so players can return, resolve unfinished business, or leave the raid area cleanly.

Raid ruleGuide note
Broadcast startRaid access begins when the raid message appears.
Event-world areaThe boss is fought in the raid/event area rather than directly on the normal map.
Depot rewardsRaid rewards are delivered to the depot when applicable.
Party setupPlayers keep their party setup when entering raid fights.
Retro OutfitsRaid Bosses such as Dracona, Abaldar, Ildar, Kavdros, and Avuria have been tied to Retro Outfit rewards.
ArtifactsRaid Boss kills are used by artifact progression in newer progression rules.
05

World Bosses

Portal bosses summoned through altar sacrifices

World Bosses use a portal and altar setup. Inside the World Boss area, players collect required items from underlings, place the items on altars, and summon the current boss when the requirements are met.

The classic World Boss rotation includes Abaldar, Avuria, Dracona, Ildar, and Kavdros. Each boss has its own underlings, altar items, and unique reward possibilities.

06

Ethereal, Island, and Elite Bosses

Additional boss reward sources connected to Soul Shards

Some boss categories are mainly important because they are tied to higher-end reward systems. Ethereal Bosses, Island Bosses, Elite Creatures, Wild Bosses, World Bosses, and Raid Bosses are all grouped as Soul Shard sources.

Ethereal and Island boss routes are best handled on their related area pages, while this page keeps the boss reward context together.

Boss sourceKnown use
Ethereal BossesMajor Soul Shard source.
Island BossesMajor Soul Shard source.
Elite CreaturesIncluded with boss-style Soul Shard sources.
Wargate BossesWargate boss kills count toward Wargate progress and have also been tied into Wild Boss achievement progress.
Seasonal bossesEvent bosses such as Demon Oak or Permafrost Queen belong on the Events Hub, but can be linked from this boss page.
