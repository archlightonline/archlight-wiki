Bounty System

Timed buffs from the top-right menu

Bounties are found in the top-right menu. They provide timed hunting buffs such as loot drop, monster essence, experience, gold drop, and loot slime. Each bounty lasts for 2 hours.

01🎯

Roll or select

Roll for a monster using Bounty Cards or use the select option.

02🔓

Unlock slots

Start with 1 slot. Each additional slot costs 150 Bounty Cards as a one-time cost.

03🔁

Reroll details

Reroll the type or amount of the bounty bonus.

04⏳

Lock the bonus

Locking a bonus automatically extends duration once expired.
