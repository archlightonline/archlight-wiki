Activity Overview

What Endless Abyss is used for

Endless Abyss is a daily farming activity focused on high experience. Players spend a limited creature pool during a timed run, choosing a difficulty tier that affects the experience gained per creature.

⚔

High Experience Farm

Difficulty tiers determine the experience gained per creature. Available difficulty tiers range from Easy through Extreme.

Experience FocusTier Based
🕳

Daily Creature Pool

Players receive 2,000 Endless Abyss creatures per day. Unused creatures can stack up to a maximum pool of 10,000.

💠

Loot Still Drops

Regular loot and Monster Essence still drop inside the activity. Regular loot and Monster Essence are available during runs.

02

Entry and Run Rules

Daily entry, timer, pool use, and cooldown

Endless Abyss unlocks at level 500 and can be entered once per day. A run is limited by both time and the remaining creature pool.

01🔓

Unlock at level 500

The activity becomes available once the character reaches level 500.

02📅

Enter once per day

Players receive 2,000 creatures per day and can enter once per day.

03⏱

Run for up to 30 minutes

Runs are limited to 30 minutes. The run also ends when the creature pool is depleted.

04↩

Keep unused creatures

If the run ends or the player dies, remaining creatures return to the pool instead of being lost.

05⌛

Wait 5 minutes between entries

There is a 5-minute cooldown between entries.
