Hunting Flow

Portal hunting and task stacking
01🧭

Choose a hunting tier

Use the Hunting Zones page as the combined reference for normal hunting areas and the old Monster Portals page. Monster Portal tasks are now kept here so players do not have to check two near-identical pages.

02📜

Pick matching tasks

Task NPCs can provide concurrent objectives. Pick the kill tasks before entering the matching portal or hunting area.

03⚔

Stack long-term goals

Portal tasks, addon tasks, stat tasks, daily tasks, and other creature objectives can overlap with the same monster families.

04⛓

Move into larger activities

After the basic hunting loop becomes stable, players usually branch into dungeons, rifts, wargates, Ramparts, and other repeatable content.

Monster Portal Hub

What was kept from the deleted Monster Portals page

Monster Portals were merged into Hunting Zones because both pages covered the same player flow: choosing a spawn or portal, accepting monster tasks, and using those kills for experience and progression. The standalone Monster Portals concept page was removed, but its task tables were preserved here.
