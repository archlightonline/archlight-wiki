Julieta and Prison Entry

Where the activity starts

Prisons are entered through Julieta, the Prisons NPC. Patch notes confirm a protection zone around Julieta, so the entry area is meant to give players a safer starting point before they commit to the activity.

Confirmed itemPatch-note detail
NPCJulieta is identified as the Prisons NPC.
Entry safetyA protection zone was added around Julieta.
Activity typePatch notes refer to group Prison instances and Prison runs.
02

Gold Nugget Sacrifice

Difficulty and reward scaling

Players can sacrifice multiple Gold Nuggets when entering Prisons. The amount is chosen on entrance, and the sacrifice increases both the difficulty and the reward by the amount of Gold Nuggets sacrificed.

01🪙

Choose the sacrifice

Pick the amount of Gold Nuggets at the entrance.

02🔥

Raise the difficulty

More sacrificed Gold Nuggets make the Prison harder.

03🎁

Raise the reward

The same sacrifice also increases reward potential.

04🛡

Scale carefully

Use an amount your character can clear consistently instead of overcommitting.

03

Inside the Prison

Lockdowns, protection, and boss pressure

Prison runs include pressure mechanics and boss tuning. The latest confirmed lockdown values in the patch notes reduce the Prison Lockdown trigger chance to 50% and reduce its duration to 15 seconds.

RuleConfirmed value or behavior
Prison Lockdown chance50% trigger chance.
Prison Lockdown duration15 seconds.
Boss dangerPrison bosses received major health and damage tuning in Legacy notes, followed by a later 30% damage reduction.

Prison Rewards

Rucksacks, Glyph Tokens, and bonus drops

Patch notes confirm Prison reward value through rucksacks, Glyph Tokens, magnets, and Gold Nugget sacrifice scaling. Prison rucksacks have a rare chance to drop Glyph Tokens, and a later note increased the chance and amount of Glyph Tokens from Prison rucksacks.
