01

Ramparts Siege

Open-instance PvP activity with power brackets

Ramparts Siege is a recurring open-instance activity with PvP enabled. Instances are separated by power brackets so players compete inside a bracket closer to their character strength.

01⚔

Enter on active days

Ramparts runs on a 3-day cycle.

02⏱

Watch the warning

Players receive a 5-minute warning before the activity begins.

03🏰

Clear the instance

Monsters inside do not respawn.

04💀

Push bosses

Bosses are the reward checkpoints, with a final chest after all bosses fall.

03

Inside Ramparts

Boss path, random areas, and monster rules

Patch notes describe Ramparts as a shorter boss path with themed boss maps. All bosses except the first and last are random. When a boss dies, a teleport appears on its corpse and leads to the next random area, with the next area scaling stronger.

Inside ruleHow it works
Monster respawnsMonsters inside do not respawn.
Boss sequenceFirst and last bosses are fixed, middle bosses are random.
Teleport flowAfter a boss dies, a teleport spawns on its corpse.
ScalingThe next area scales stronger.
Mother WormMother Worm became a regular random boss; spawn frequency reduced from every 2% health to every 5%, and summons reduced from 8 to 5.
Ramparts WormLater changelog removed ranged damage from Ramparts Worm.
04

Rewards

Boss rewards, final chest, and currency

Any player who deals damage to a boss receives rewards when that boss is defeated, limited to once per day per character per boss. After all bosses fall, a final reward chest appears once per day per character, requiring at least one boss kill. Rewards scale based on how many bosses were defeated.

RewardRequirement or value
Boss rewardDeal damage to the boss before it dies. Once per day per character per boss.
Final chestAppears after all bosses fall. Once per day per character and requires at least one boss kill.
Scaling reward valueRewards scale based on number of bosses defeated.
Boss currency10 Ramparts Currency Tokens per boss kill, listed as 120 total tokens per Ramparts.
Last boss bonus+30 Tokens for completing the Last Boss.
