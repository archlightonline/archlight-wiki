Rifts Overview

Group activity, Rift Knife access, and Rift Lord progress

Rifts are repeatable combat instances used for progression, monster farming, and Rift-related objectives. The current verified rules list Rifts as a limited-player activity, with Rift player count set to 7 players.

01🌀

Enter the Rift

Join the Rift when eligible and prepare for a shared combat instance.

02👥

Play within the limit

Rift player count is listed as 7 players in the latest available rule set.

03🗡

Use the Rift Knife source

Rift Knife can be purchased from Darkness Lestrage for 3,000 Archlight Tokens, or obtained through Dimensional Minerva’s Crypt for free.

04🏆

Progress Rift Lords

Defeating 10 Rift Lords is listed as a Talent Point milestone worth 3 points.

Rewards and Progression Hooks

Glyph Tokens, Rift Lords, and related currencies

Rifts connect into several progression systems. Patch-note sources confirm Glyph Token drops from Rift monsters, stronger monsters having better chances, and a Rift Lord milestone for Talent Tree progress.

04

Player Notes

How to approach Rift runs
  1. Bring a stable build. Rifts are group combat content, so survivability and consistent damage are safer than only burst.
  2. Check your Rift Knife access. If you lost or still need access, use the confirmed Darkness Lestrage purchase route or the Dimensional Minerva’s Crypt route.
  3. Prioritize stronger Rift monsters when farming Glyph Tokens. The stronger-monster note means high-value kills matter more than only kill count.
  4. Track Rift Lord progress. Rift Lord kills can matter for account progression when Talent Tree milestones are enabled.
