01

Secret Map System

Instanced and hidden dungeon maps

Secret Maps are dungeon-like activities that reward exclusive items. There are two types: Instanced Dungeons and Hidden Dungeons. Maps can be done solo or in group, and map rarity affects reward quality.

01📖

Open a book

Ancient Books produce a random book rarity, and books can produce maps of the same or rarer tier.

02🗺

Use the map

The map points to a setup spot in the Archlight world or sends the player to a Legendary Map dungeon.

03👥

Sync group maps

Group maps can prompt nearby party members to sync their map location.

04☠

Clear and spawn boss

After all monsters die, the boss spawns after 10 seconds near the player.

03

Map Fusion

Enhancing map rarity

Maps can be fused by using one map to enhance and sacrificing other maps of the same rarity for a chance to upgrade to a higher rarity. For example, sacrificing one Epic Map gives 33% chance to enhance another Epic Map to Legendary, while sacrificing two gives 66%.
