Wargate Rules

Portal drop, challenger rules, and completion flow

Wargates are 1v1 PvP and PvE portal instances that can appear while hunting creatures in the world. Each Wargate generates a map with creatures, traps, chests, bosses, Power Ups, and Affixes scaled around the player’s strength or power bracket.

01🌀

Find a Wargate

Kill creatures in the world for a chance to gain access to a Wargate.

02⚔

Enter the instance

The gate creates a combat map with monsters, traps, chests, and a boss.

03👤

Watch for a challenger

A second player can appear as a gate-crusader, matched within your power bracket.

04🏆

Clear and claim

Fight through the gate, defeat the boss, and claim the Wargates Currency reward.

Currency and Shop

Wargates Currency spending and bonuses

Wargates use Wargates Currency as their main reward. The Wargate NPC is located upstairs from depot, and older shop references include progression and utility rewards.

06

Player Notes

How to play gates cleanly
  1. Clear with awareness. Wargates are not only monster rooms, traps and affixes can punish tunnel vision.
  2. Use Power Ups quickly. Absorbing a useful Power Up before a challenger fight or boss pull can decide the run.
  3. Do not ignore the opponent. When another player enters, the gate becomes a resource fight over monsters, chests, boss control, and reward claim.
  4. Spend currency intentionally. Wargates Currency connects to utility and progression shop rewards, so avoid wasting it before checking current shop prices.
