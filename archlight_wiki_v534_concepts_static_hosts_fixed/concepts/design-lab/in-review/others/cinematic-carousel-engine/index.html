Home Module Concept

Moment Atlas

A cinematic, wide home module for in-game screenshots and short clips. It should feel like a living campaign board inside the wiki, not a square carousel block.

Featured moment

The gates open

Launch crowds, early routes, first tasks, and the screenshots that make an era feel alive.

Where it fits

Place it below the social media buttons on Home as one panoramic story strip.

How content stays simple

Each slide needs only a title, short line, category, and media source. The module handles the cinematic framing.

Why it fits a wiki

It promotes community moments without turning Home into a heavy media wall or noisy social feed.
