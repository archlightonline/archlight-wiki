Bestiary Overview

Kill tracking and permanent rewards

The Bestiary tracks monster kills and rewards progress through milestones. Players earn Bestiary Points and experience from monster milestones, then spend Bestiary Points on permanent buffs, items, and effects.

Bestiary Shop

Confirmed reward examples

The Bestiary interface lets players spend Bestiary Points. The Legacy 2025 notes state that each listed reward costs 100 Bestiary Points. Most rewards can only be purchased once, while +1 All Stats is repeatable.

05

Unlockable Titles

Achievement-based bonuses

Legacy 2025 notes list golden title unlocks through Bestiary achievements, with permanent bonuses attached. Examples include Lore Keeper for 2,500 kills on any monster type and Harbinger of Darkness for 10,000 Darkness monster kills.
