Trait Overview

Always-active vocation identity

Vocation Traits are always-active passive effects that define each vocation role. They are organized into three layers: Base Vocation Traits, Specialization Traits, and Prestige Amplifiers. Party-wide traits do not stack with themselves, and traits are displayed through an icon near the health bar.
