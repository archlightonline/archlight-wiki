Profession Codex

Side menu with one readable guide body per profession

Professions are a long-term progression layer for gathering materials, crafting useful items, earning Achievement Points, and supporting Guild Island progression. The current system uses profession energy, upgradeable soulbound tools, profession-specific materials, crafting stations, and daily tasks, so gathering and crafting both matter during the season.

Profession System

Overview and guide flow

Overview

In vast world of Archlight there are many Professions that will help you get your character to the top of the ladder.

They can be divided into two categories: Gathering Professions and Crafting Professions. You will get powerful bonuses to you character from Enchancements and Stat Bonuses once you will reach level 50 in each of 6 Crafting Professions. To level them up you will need ALOT of materials so get ready to do some serious gathering sessions (unless of course you're a millionaire, then you can buy all the materials off the market).

While rewards from crafting are much more powerful, all professions will grant you lots of Achievement Points that will help you improve your movement speed, loot chance, or even increase experience gained! So in the end spending some time on gathering might be really worthwhile for everyone, to boost ones AP.

AP's are additive so at level 50 of each profession you will get 55 AP overall (from a single profession).

Current Profession System

  • Tools: Each profession uses one soulbound tool line upgraded through tiers such as Iron, Steel, Obsidian, Hellsteel, and Dragon.
  • Energy: Energy starts at 2,500 capacity and gains +250 per Prestige. Crafting and gathering consume energy based on the action or recipe complexity.
  • Material Sorting: Players can assign backpacks to profession materials through right-click sorting.

Daily Energy Sources

  • +25 Energy from completing Daily Tasks
  • +100 Energy from Dungeon or Quest completion
  • +1,000 Energy from Zaqor’s Tower
  • +200/300/500 Energy from Guild Plots (based on tier)
  • 500% Energy Regain Booster from Bounty Boosts (10 cards for 12 hours)

Specialization and Stations

  • Specializations: professions level to 50 before specialization becomes the major progression layer. Players can choose two specializations, with one mastered specialization applying the Craftsman Perk.
  • Crafting Time: some recipes use crafting time instead of instant crafting. Time can be sped up with gold and Archlight Tokens.
  • Stations: depot stations support early crafting, while house and Guild Island stations unlock higher station tiers and specialization support.
  • Rare Materials: examples include Magic Quartz, Celestial Ore Vein, Saffron, Legendary Fish, Cinnabar materials, and Enchanted Trees.

These are written as current guide rules from the Updates and patch-note archive, not as changelog history.

AreaCurrent guide detail
Profession windowThe profession UI is accessed from the top Character menu and shows profession level progress, current bonuses, station access, material locations, and the tool used for each material.
StationsInside a house or Guild Island, the profession window can access stations available in that location. Depot stations only go up to profession level 10.
ToolsEach gathering profession uses an upgradeable tool line. Players progress the tool from Iron through Dragon instead of carrying separate old tool items.
Energy capMaximum profession energy is 2,500. Each Prestige level grants +250 maximum energy.
Energy from itemsEnergy gained from items has a daily limit of +2,000, excluding beds.
Activity energyDaily tasks grant +25 energy, dungeons grant +100 energy, quests grant +100 energy, and Zaqor’s Tower grants +1,000 energy.
Material assignmentBackpacks can be assigned to profession material categories. Later material assignment support also added an Others section for Slime Materials.
Daily profession tasksDaily tasks are based on earning profession experience from any source. Completing the daily task grants extra experience and a 10% future craft experience bonus for the day.
SpecializationCrafting professions can specialize after level 50. The specialization level is the level used for Craftsman perk checks.
