Race Points Overview

Account-wide race reward currency

Race Points are the current race reward model. Race objectives and conditions stay the same, but completing races grants Race Points instead of fixed rewards.

Races are no longer limited. Players can participate as many times as they wish.

02

Earning Race Points

Reward logic
  • Earlier completions grant significantly more points.
  • Some races have a soft cap where later participants receive a smaller fixed amount.
  • Race objectives and conditions are unchanged.
